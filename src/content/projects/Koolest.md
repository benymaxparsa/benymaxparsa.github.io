- Designed and built a replacement home screen for Android TV and Google TV in Kotlin, shipped as a single 0.7 MB APK with no third-party libraries, no ads, no account and no tracking.
- Liquid Glass interface in the style of iOS 26: a glass menu, a billboard that fills with the focused item's art, and rows for Continue watching, apps, consoles and streaming services.
- Reads Android TV's Watch Next list and apps' home-screen channels to build a Continue watching row that works across apps.
- Detects consoles and devices over HDMI-CEC (PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, Apple TV and more) and switches the TV to their input in one click.
- Makes the Home button open Koolest even on Google TV, where the system launcher can't be replaced, and does the adb setup from the remote through the TV's own network debugging.
- Gallery screensaver with paintings, clock, weather and now playing; available in English, French and Persian, on any brand running Android 8 to 16.
